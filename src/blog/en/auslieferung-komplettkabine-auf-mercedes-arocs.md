## Delivery of a 5.9m Living Cabin with Electric Storage Box Staircase on Mercedes Arocs 2042 4x4

A coherent travel vehicle from front to back for long-term travel with a dog was recently handed over to the owners. The warm interior with cosy round seating area and plenty of equipment is made to become a new home.

## Living Cabin

The living cabin was built according to the FAS principle with a massive self-supporting steel frame and floating GRP sandwich panels. With 70mm thick outer walls, the cabin is exceptionally well insulated.

## Interior

The warm muted colours in the interior were combined with eye-catchers in red Alcantara. The customers chose buffalo leather in sand colour for the round seating area, and the lava-look kitchen worktop complements the successful combination of earthy tones with elegant modern accents.

The cabin was equipped for the first time with a special smart home control system for expedition vehicles.

### Seating Area

Right at the entrance, the generous round seating area invites you to relax. The cushion in front of the pass-through to the driver's cab is removable, making passage easy for both humans and pets.

### Kitchen and Hallway

The 1.45m long kitchen worktop features a 2-plate induction hob and a scratch-resistant BLANCO sink in volcanic grey. The worktop has a foldable extension creating a generous total length of 2m.

### Bathroom/WC

The combined shower room features an OGO composting toilet, a ceramic washbasin and a shower tray on 1.2m length. A special highlight is the installed Shower-Loop, which recycles and reheats the shower water.

### Sleeping Area

The generous double bed measuring 2.05 x 2.0m offers plenty of space for retreat. Two foldable solid oak step surfaces facilitate the climb into bed. Behind them hides the 3kg TINY WASH washer-dryer.

## Exterior

### Base Vehicle Preparations

A wheelbase extension of 30cm was carried out on the Arocs according to Mercedes Benz body guidelines. A fuel tank system with a total capacity of 610L was installed.

### Exterior Staircase, Outdoor Kitchen and Storage Boxes

The storage box staircase with side access was electrified for the first time for this vehicle. A stainless steel outdoor kitchen was installed in the rear passenger-side storage box.

### Rear Carrier

The mounted Rear Carrier XL is manufactured in proven construction. The double telescopic extension allows lowering to ground level. Self-weight approx. 400kg, payload 875kg.

### Roof Rack

The custom-made roof rack not only gives the vehicle a balanced appearance. The galvanised and chassis-colour powder-coated construction carries 2 powerful light bars. Access to the Permateek-decked roof terrace is via the pneumatically extendable ladder.

We wish our customers all the best and safe travels always!
