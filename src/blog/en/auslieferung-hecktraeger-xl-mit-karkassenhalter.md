## Rear Carrier XL for MAN 18.430

Another Rear Carrier XL was manufactured for the MAN TGS with Kerkamm body. With the 1,010mm long platform, loading options are kept flexible. The platform can be lowered to the ground using the cable remote control. This carrier also features 3 different locking positions. A powerful electric winch ensures reliable raising and lowering of loads up to 875kg.

In addition to the complete spare wheel, a tyre without rim is also to travel along. A carcass holder including matching ratchet straps can be ordered specifically for this purpose. The rimless spare tyre should be fitted with an inner tube by the customer to prevent water ingress.

The matching FAS underbody protection is approved according to ECE58R03.

At the customer's request, the colour scheme of the expedition vehicle was carried over to the rear carrier.

We wish our customers safe travels always!
