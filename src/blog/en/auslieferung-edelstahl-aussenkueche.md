## FAS Outdoor Kitchen for Action Mobil Global X

We were once again able to custom-adapt the FAS outdoor kitchen for an Action Mobil. The stainless steel construction was adapted to the existing storage box. The customer's own contact grill and both induction hobs were fitted into the kitchen and permanently mounted. Thanks to the storage option on the 2nd level, enough usable work surface remains when needed.

Due to space constraints, one wing was fixed while the second wing remains foldable.

The FAS outdoor kitchen has standard dimensions of approx. L540 x D510 x H540 mm (folded). It normally has two foldable side wings and is equipped with a mini sink with spray, a contact grill and an induction hob. Adaptation to existing storage boxes and customer appliances is possible at any time and only requires measuring the storage box. The outdoor kitchen can also be shipped for self-assembly.
