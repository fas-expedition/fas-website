## Again in 2025 at Booth T02

Dear customers, interested parties and partners,

We would like to cordially invite you to visit us at this year's Abenteuer Allrad trade fair in Bad Kissingen from June 19th to 22nd. At our joint booth T02 with the Unimog general distributor RKF-Bleses GmbH, you can expect:

- A complete vehicle based on the Mercedes Benz Arocs 6x6 with a 7.4m living cabin
  - Highlight: The living cabin is currently being converted and offers unique insights into the technical components for energy, water, and air conditioning
- Two empty FAS cabins on the Mercedes Unimog U4023 and U5023

You can also enjoy the opportunity to see:

- All of our modular rear carrier platforms
- The stylish FAS roof rack
- The newly developed central tube subframe

in person.

The entire FAS team is looking forward to your visit!
