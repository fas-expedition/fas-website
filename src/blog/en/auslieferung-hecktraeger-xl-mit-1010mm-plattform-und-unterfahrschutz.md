## Rear Carrier XL on Mercedes Benz Arocs 2036

Maximum design freedom for loading is offered by the carrier platform for this Mercedes Arocs 2036.

The base module provides mounting options for 2 complete spare wheels up to max. 14.00R20. The bolt-on platform features an aluminium floor whose hole pattern keeps all options open for individual loading.

The FAS underbody protection is foldable and complies with the current EU regulation ECE58R03.

The vehicle by manufacturer Kashmir Overlander can be viewed at their booth C18 at the Abenteuer Allrad Fair 2025 in Bad Kissingen.
